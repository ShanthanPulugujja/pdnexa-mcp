## Before you write

- Confirm you are signed in with an **existing** pdnexa account. Google does not auto-create one.
- Check `mcp_quota` for the current UTC month. Free accounts can complete **3 jobs** in that month.
- Paid uncapping is managed only on [https://pdnexa.com](https://pdnexa.com), not in the Cursor Marketplace or Grok Bot Plugins.
- Download links expire about **30 minutes** after the job is created. Use `mcp_job_status` with the job id while the link is still valid.

This listing does not bill you. Installing the plugin is free.
